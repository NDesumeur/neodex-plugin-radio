# Radio

French web radios live, in an old pixel-art radio set.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

- 26 stations by genre: news and culture, music, rap, rock, jazz, classical.
- **Space**: play or stop · **up and down**: station · **F**: favourite · **+ and −**: volume.
- A sleep timer stops the radio after 15 min to 1 h 30.

## World radio (new in 1.2.0)

Search Radio-Browser (over 50,000 stations): name, city or style; stations you play join your list. The radio keeps playing with its tab closed.

## Permissions

Since version 1.2.0, Radio asks for network access to all sites: world stations come from thousands of different servers. Audio comes straight from each station's server, and the search goes through Radio-Browser (api.radio-browser.info). Nothing else is sent.

## World radio

The **World radio** box searches Radio-Browser, the free directory of over 50,000 stations: a name, a city or a style (jazz, Tokyo, BBC…). Only working https stations are shown, most liked first. A station you play joins the "World radio" genre in your list (the last 30; your favourites stay). The radio keeps playing when you close its tab.


## Version 1.1.0

- One readable card per station: its name, its genre, and the current track for the one you are listening to.
- Current track (song and artist, or show) for the 17 Radio France stations that provide it, refreshed every 30 seconds; it scrolls on the radio's screen.
