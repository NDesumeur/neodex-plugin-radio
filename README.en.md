# Radio

French web radios live, in an old pixel-art radio set.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

- 26 stations by genre: news and culture, music, rap, rock, jazz, classical.
- **Space**: play or stop · **up and down**: station · **F**: favourite · **+ and −**: volume.
- A sleep timer stops the radio after 15 min to 1 h 30.

## Permissions

Audio comes straight from the stations’ servers: icecast.radiofrance.fr, *.ice.infomaniak.ch and icecast.skyrock.net.

## Version 1.1.0

- One readable card per station: its name, its genre, and the current track for the one you are listening to.
- Current track (song and artist, or show) for the 17 Radio France stations that provide it, refreshed every 30 seconds; it scrolls on the radio's screen.
